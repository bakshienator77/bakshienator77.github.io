I build learning systems for robots and lead teams that turn research into working products.

I'm the Founding ML Engineer at Tuesday Labs, building a delightful bimanual bipedal home robot. My work spans building the end-to-end reinforcement learning pipeline for character-consistent locomotion, behaviour switching, and expressive robot behavior to truly bring our robot to life.

At Arena Physica, I led development of an AI hardware engineer and built multimodal representation-learning systems. At Carnegie Mellon, I developed algorithms for heterogeneous multi-robot active search under uncertainty.

I hold an M.S. in Robotics from CMU and a B.Tech. in Mechanical Engineering with a Computer Science minor from IIT Kharagpur.

At CMU's Robotics Institute and National Robotics Engineering Center, I worked on decentralized search with heterogeneous robots, noisy observations, and unreliable communication.

GUTS: Generalized Uncertainty-Aware Thompson Sampling for Multi-Agent Active Search

Nikhil Angad Bakshi, Tejus Gupta, Ramina Ghods, Jeff Schneider

ICRA 2023 · Outstanding Deployed Systems Paper

Helping teams of robots decide where to search next under uncertainty. I developed GUTS to connect uncertainty-aware planning with multi-robot active search. The implementation is included in the STAR repository as a state-of-the-art baseline for comparison.

Stealthy Terrain-Aware Multi-Agent Active Search

Nikhil Angad Bakshi, Jeff Schneider

CoRL 2023

Finding targets while remaining concealed. I developed STAR to use terrain information to balance search efficiency with the risk of revealing a robot's location.
